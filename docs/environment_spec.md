# Canonical environment specification

The active contract is `heterogeneous_mavuav_4v4_v3_3`: one armed MAV and three armed Red UAVs versus four Blue-team UAVs controlled by a homogeneous fixed rule. Entity order is `MAV, UAV1, UAV2, UAV3, Blue1, Blue2, Blue3, Blue4`. Red and Blue UAVs share exactly the same speed and overload limits; MAV remains the distinct high-performance heterogeneous platform. `Blue` remains a team/slot/type-encoding label and does not denote a third flight-performance class. Same-type Red/Blue UAV means identical flight dynamics and maneuver envelope, not symmetric information. MAV loss is immediate Red mission failure; UAV loss alone does not terminate an episode.

Dynamics and combat semantics are unchanged from the validated predecessor: overload-controlled 3DOF motion, trim-centred normalized actions, 1 s decisions, 0.1 s RK4 physics, 75 decisions, 1--3 km engagement distance, strict ATA below 30 degrees, strict AA below 90 degrees, three consecutive decision steps, pairwise streaks and synchronous kill resolution. Each Blue independently targets the nearest alive Red aircraft, including MAV, using true-state distance with stable `RED_IDS` tie-breaking. Its deterministic 27-action one-step greedy controller retains situation-reward scoring and boundary/recovery filtering. Blue is deliberately a privileged-state benchmark opponent: it has no sensor, search, last-known-target or datalink state. The horizontal +/-100 km battlefield is a non-binding safety envelope, not a claimed 200 km tactical engagement region.

Nominal starts in metres are MAV `(-5000,0,5000)`, UAV1 `(-4000,-1200,5000)`, UAV2 `(-4000,0,5000)`, UAV3 `(-4000,1200,5000)`, Blue1 `(4000,-1800,5000)`, Blue2 `(4000,-600,5000)`, Blue3 `(4000,600,5000)`, and Blue4 `(4000,1800,5000)`. Red heads 0 degrees and Blue heads 180 degrees. All eight aircraft start nominally at 275 m/s, the midpoint of the common feasible interval `[250,300]`; main-profile +/-20 m/s jitter therefore remains strictly inside every platform's limits. The MAV is 1 km behind the UAV front, and even opposing +/-300 m slot offsets leave at least 400 m rear separation. Red/Blue UAV limits are `v=[150,300] m/s`, `nx=[-1,5]`, `ny=[-1.5,1.5]`, `nz=[-2,2]`; MAV limits remain `v=[250,400] m/s`, `nx=[-1,5]`, `ny=[-1.5,2]`, `nz=[-3,3]`.

Relative to v3.2, v3.3 has exactly two behavioral changes: initial geometry/kinematics changes from a 0.5 km MAV rear offset and 325/225 m/s MAV/UAV speeds to a 1 km rear offset and common 275 m/s speed; Blue maneuver targeting changes from nearest living Red UAV with MAV fallback to nearest living Red aircraft. The old 100 m/s relative speed encoded a nominal 5 s MAV catch-up. The new nominal relative speed is zero, so no initial-speed catch-up is encoded. At the 8 km nominal UAV/Blue longitudinal separation, head-on closure to the 3 km attack maximum changes only from about 11.1 s at 225+225 m/s to about 9.1 s at 275+275 m/s.

Unchanged choices are MAV/UAV sensing at 12/8 km, ideal instantaneous Red datalink, privileged-state fixed-rule Blue, aircraft performance limits, 5 km common nominal altitude, combat geometry and hold, horizon, reward, termination and MAV attack capability. The armed MAV is this project's scenario choice, not a claim that it reproduces Chen et al.'s unarmed MAV.

## Actor observation

Each Red actor receives 100 values. Layout is self `[0:11]`; three friend blocks `[11:22]`, `[22:33]`, `[33:44]` in stable `RED_IDS` order while skipping self; and Blue blocks `[44:58]`, `[58:72]`, `[72:86]`, `[86:100]`. Self is `[x,y,h,v,theta,psi,alive,type_MAV,type_UAV,type_Blue,time]`. Each friend is `[dx,dy,dh,distance,dvx,dvy,dvh,alive,type_MAV,type_UAV,type_Blue]`. Each enemy is `[dx,dy,dh,distance,dvx,dvy,dvh,ATA,AA,alive,direct_visible,datalink_visible,own_attack_streak,killed_by_red]`.

## Centralized state

The critic receives 117 values: eight 10D entity blocks `[0:80]`; 32 normalized directed cross-team attack streaks `[80:112]` ordered as all Red-to-Blue pairs followed by all Blue-to-Red pairs; four Red-kill-history flags `[112:116]`; and time fraction `[116]`. The active mask order is the four Red slots.

## Reward and termination

For each currently team-visible alive Blue, the reward uses the best five-part situation score achieved by any alive Red, then averages those scores across the visible Blue aircraft. It is zero when no alive Red or no visible alive Blue exists. Event, terminal, safety, sensor/datalink and normalization semantics and coefficients are unchanged. A Red win requires all four Blue aircraft to have been destroyed by Red attacks while MAV remains alive.

HAPPO uses four independent actors; MAPPO uses one shared actor over four Red samples. R-HAPPO uses four independent GRUs. HRTA and Structured Uniform parse three friend blocks and four enemy blocks. v3.3 checkpoints carry the unchanged 100D/117D layout plus the revised foundation semantics and intentionally reject v3.2 and older checkpoints.
