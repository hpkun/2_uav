# Canonical environment specification

The final frozen contract is `heterogeneous_mavuav_4v4_v3_5`: one armed MAV and three armed Red UAVs versus four Blue-team UAVs controlled by a homogeneous fixed rule. Entity order is `MAV, UAV1, UAV2, UAV3, Blue1, Blue2, Blue3, Blue4`. Red and Blue UAVs share exactly the same speed and overload limits; MAV remains the distinct high-performance heterogeneous platform. `Blue` remains a team/slot/type-encoding label and does not denote a third flight-performance class. Same-type Red/Blue UAV means identical flight dynamics and maneuver envelope, not symmetric information. MAV loss is immediate Red mission failure; UAV loss alone does not terminate an episode.

Dynamics and combat semantics are unchanged from the validated predecessor: overload-controlled 3DOF motion, trim-centred normalized actions, 1 s decisions, 0.1 s RK4 physics, 75 decisions, 1--3 km engagement distance, strict ATA below 30 degrees, strict AA below 90 degrees, three consecutive decision steps, pairwise streaks and synchronous kill resolution. Each Blue independently targets the nearest alive Red aircraft, including MAV, using direct true-state squared distance with stable `RED_IDS` tie-breaking. At decision steps 0, 2, 4, ... it caches the heading and pitch to that target's current position and tracks those cached angles for two decisions; target death, invalid cache, or recovery exit forces immediate refresh. It does not cache a target position and recompute line of sight on the held step. Achievable turn rates are clipped using the existing 3DOF overload envelope, speed derivative is set to zero, and overloads are exactly inverse-mapped to trim-centred normalized actions. Altitude safety uses the analytic recovery guard and fixed maximum recovery action; horizontal safety uses emergency steer-to-centre. No candidate search, predictive integration, or situation reward is used in Blue action selection.

The two-step guidance cache is deterministic opponent-controller internal state. It is saved in vector/trainer checkpoints for exact continuation but is not added to Red observation or global state.

Nominal starts in metres are MAV `(-5000,0,5000)`, UAV1 `(-4000,-1200,5000)`, UAV2 `(-4000,0,5000)`, UAV3 `(-4000,1200,5000)`, Blue1 `(4000,-1800,5000)`, Blue2 `(4000,-600,5000)`, Blue3 `(4000,600,5000)`, and Blue4 `(4000,1800,5000)`. Red heads 0 degrees and Blue heads 180 degrees. All eight aircraft start nominally at 275 m/s, the midpoint of the common feasible interval `[250,300]`; main-profile +/-20 m/s jitter therefore remains strictly inside every platform's limits. The MAV is 1 km behind the UAV front, and even opposing +/-300 m slot offsets leave at least 400 m rear separation. Red/Blue UAV limits are `v=[150,300] m/s`, `nx=[-1,5]`, `ny=[-1.5,1.5]`, `nz=[-2,2]`; MAV limits remain `v=[250,400] m/s`, `nx=[-1,5]`, `ny=[-1.5,2]`, `nz=[-3,3]`.

Relative to v3.4, v3.5 has exactly one scientific behavioral change: Blue guidance changes from every-step real-time pure pursuit to a two-decision periodic heading/pitch hold. Nearest-alive-Red target selection is unchanged. The reason is not to tune Red win rate: the formal v3.4 2M experiment produced 0% Red wins, zero Red kills and 100% draws, exposing a structural coupling between perfect nose-on tracking and the distance/ATA/AA/hold attack gate. Periodic rule guidance avoids that degenerate geometry while retaining a deterministic, privileged-state, computationally light opponent. Dynamics, parameters, initialization, sensing/datalink, reward, attack, termination and observation/state contracts are unchanged.

Unchanged choices are MAV/UAV sensing at 12/8 km, ideal instantaneous Red datalink, privileged-state fixed-rule Blue, aircraft performance limits, 5 km common nominal altitude, combat geometry and hold, horizon, reward, termination and MAV attack capability. The armed MAV is this project's scenario choice, not a claim that it reproduces Chen et al.'s unarmed MAV.

## Actor observation

Each Red actor receives 100 values. Layout is self `[0:11]`; three friend blocks `[11:22]`, `[22:33]`, `[33:44]` in stable `RED_IDS` order while skipping self; and Blue blocks `[44:58]`, `[58:72]`, `[72:86]`, `[86:100]`. Self is `[x,y,h,v,theta,psi,alive,type_MAV,type_UAV,type_Blue,time]`. Each friend is `[dx,dy,dh,distance,dvx,dvy,dvh,alive,type_MAV,type_UAV,type_Blue]`. Each enemy is `[dx,dy,dh,distance,dvx,dvy,dvh,ATA,AA,alive,direct_visible,datalink_visible,own_attack_streak,killed_by_red]`.

## Centralized state

The critic receives 117 values: eight 10D entity blocks `[0:80]`; 32 normalized directed cross-team attack streaks `[80:112]` ordered as all Red-to-Blue pairs followed by all Blue-to-Red pairs; four Red-kill-history flags `[112:116]`; and time fraction `[116]`. The active mask order is the four Red slots.

## Reward and termination

For each currently team-visible alive Blue, the reward uses the best five-part situation score achieved by any alive Red, then averages those scores across the visible Blue aircraft. It is zero when no alive Red or no visible alive Blue exists. Event, terminal, safety, sensor/datalink and normalization semantics and coefficients are unchanged. A Red win requires all four Blue aircraft to have been destroyed by Red attacks while MAV remains alive.

HAPPO uses four independent actors; MAPPO uses one shared actor over four Red samples. R-HAPPO uses four independent GRUs. HRTA, Structured Uniform and PCTA parse three friend blocks and four enemy blocks. v3.5 checkpoints carry the unchanged 100D/117D layout plus the periodic-guidance Blue semantics and intentionally reject v3.4 and older checkpoints.
